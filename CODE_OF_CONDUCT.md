# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, developers, maintainers, and users of the **JSView Jewelry ERP** project pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or any other personal characteristic.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, respectful, and professional project environment.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our project include:

* Demonstrating empathy, professionalism, and kindness toward other contributors and users.
* Being respectful of different opinions, technical approaches, viewpoints, and experiences.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
* Collaborating openly when discussing software architecture, database design, APIs, UI/UX, ERP workflows, and business requirements.
* Accepting responsibility for mistakes and working constructively to resolve them.
* Respecting project decisions made by maintainers and authorized project administrators.
* Focusing on what is best for the JSView Jewelry ERP project and its users.
* Providing clear and useful information when reporting bugs, suggesting features, or submitting code changes.
* Respecting confidential business information, customer information, source code, credentials, and other private project data.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, or sexual attention or advances of any kind.
* Trolling, insulting, threatening, or derogatory comments.
* Personal, political, discriminatory, or abusive attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing or sharing another person's private information, such as a physical address, email address, phone number, credentials, or other personal information, without explicit permission.
* Sharing confidential customer, vendor, employee, financial, or business information without authorization.
* Intentionally introducing malicious code, unauthorized access mechanisms, or security vulnerabilities into the project.
* Deliberately disrupting project services, repositories, APIs, databases, development environments, or other project infrastructure.
* Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate or unprofessional in a software development or business environment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers and authorized administrators are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards described in this Code of Conduct.

Project maintainers may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior that they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, harmful, or inconsistent with this Code of Conduct.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct. This may include:

* Source-code commits and pull requests
* Issues and bug reports
* Feature requests
* Documentation
* Database or configuration changes
* Wiki content
* Comments and discussions
* Other contributions to the JSView Jewelry ERP project

Where appropriate, maintainers will communicate the reasons for moderation or enforcement decisions.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all official JSView Jewelry ERP project spaces.

This includes, but is not limited to:

* Source-code repositories
* Issue trackers
* Pull requests
* Project documentation
* Project communication channels
* Development and testing environments
* Official meetings and events
* Official project social-media or communication accounts

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is officially representing the JSView Jewelry ERP project in public spaces.

Examples of representing the project include using an official project email address, posting through an official project account, participating in an official meeting, or acting as an appointed project representative.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, threatening, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project maintainers at:

**[ narayan.z@supporthives.com ]**

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Project maintainers will make reasonable efforts to protect the privacy and security of the person reporting an incident.

Reports should include, where possible:

* A description of the incident.
* The date and approximate time of the incident.
* Relevant project location, such as an issue, pull request, discussion, or communication channel.
* Any relevant screenshots, logs, or other evidence.
* Names or usernames of people involved.

False or intentionally misleading reports may themselves be considered a violation of this Code of Conduct.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will consider the circumstances and impact of each incident when determining appropriate consequences.

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact:**
Use of inappropriate language or other behavior that is deemed unprofessional, inappropriate, or unwelcome.

**Consequence:**
A private written warning from the project maintainers explaining the nature of the violation and why the behavior was inappropriate. The individual may be asked to correct the behavior or provide an apology where appropriate.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact:**
A violation through a single serious incident or a repeated series of actions.

**Consequence:**
A formal warning with consequences for continued inappropriate behavior. The individual may be restricted from interacting with specific people, project discussions, or project communication channels for a specified period.

Continued violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact:**
A serious violation of project standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment, threats, or significant disruption of the project.

**Consequence:**
A temporary suspension from participation in project activities, including communication channels, repositories, issue trackers, discussions, or other project spaces, for a specified period.

During the suspension period, the individual may be prohibited from contacting people involved in the incident through official project channels.

Continued violations may result in a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact:**
Demonstrating a pattern of violations, sustained harassment, threats, malicious activity, serious security violations, or aggression toward individuals or groups.

**Consequence:**
A permanent ban from participation in official JSView Jewelry ERP project spaces and activities.

## Project-Specific Responsibilities

Because JSView Jewelry ERP is an enterprise application, all contributors are expected to take additional care when working with business and customer data.

Contributors must:

* Never commit passwords, API keys, tokens, or other credentials to the repository.
* Never expose customer, vendor, employee, financial, or other confidential business information.
* Avoid using production data in development or testing unless explicitly authorized.
* Follow project security and access-control requirements.
* Report security vulnerabilities privately to the project maintainers rather than publicly exposing them.
* Follow established coding, database migration, API, and review practices.
* Avoid making destructive database or production changes without appropriate authorization.
* Respect intellectual property and licensing requirements.
